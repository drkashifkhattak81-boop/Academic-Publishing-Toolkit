## ?? 1. Production Journal Templates (.docx)
Formatting a manuscript for a specific publisher takes **15 to 25 hours** of adjusting font sizes, columns, section heading roman numerals, and reference layouts.

This folder contains clean, pre-configured Microsoft Word (`.docx`) templates adhering to official publisher author guides:

* [**IEEE Transactions Two-Column Template**](./templates/IEEE_Transactions_Two_Column.docx) ? Standard 10pt Times New Roman, two-column layout with Roman numeral heading hierarchy (`I.`, `A.`, `1)`).
* [**IEEE Access Open Access Template**](./templates/IEEE_Access_Template.docx) ? Official IEEE Access styling with blue title headers, two-column grid, and biography sections.
* [**Nature Scientific Reports Template**](./templates/Nature_Scientific_Reports.docx) ? Clean single/double column layout compliant with Springer Nature submission guidelines.
* [**Elsevier Single-Column Template**](./templates/Elsevier_Single_Column.docx) ? Elsevier journal submission layout (Heliyon, Computers & Electrical Engineering, etc.).
* [**PLOS ONE Template**](./templates/PLOS_ONE_Template.docx) ? Standard multidisciplinary PLOS manuscript layout.
* [**MDPI Sensors Template**](./templates/MDPI_Sensors_Template.docx) ? MDPI generic multi-section style with structured keywords and author affiliation superscripts.

## ?? 2. PRISMA 2020 Flowchart Templates (.pptx & .svg)
Systematic literature reviews and meta-analyses require a strictly compliant **PRISMA 2020 Flow Diagram** (*Page et al., BMJ 2021*). Most templates online are locked PDFs or low-resolution images that cannot be edited cleanly.

* [**PRISMA 2020 Editable Template (.pptx)**](./prisma_flowcharts/PRISMA_2020_Template.pptx) ? Fully editable Microsoft PowerPoint and Google Slides diagram with separate shapes and text boxes. Just double-click to type your $(n = \dots)$ values!
* [**PRISMA 2020 High-Res Vector (.svg)**](./prisma_flowcharts/PRISMA_2020_Flowchart.svg) ? Crisp scalable vector graphic ready for inclusion in LaTeX, Word, or web manuscripts with 300+ DPI print quality.

## ?? 3. Desk-Reject Pre-Flight Checklist
Over **50% of submissions to top Q1 journals are rejected by the Editor-in-Chief before peer review**. The primary culprits are missing baseline comparisons, blurry figures, non-reproducible parameters, or outdated citations.

* [**Desk Reject Pre-Flight Checklist (30 Points)**](./checklists/Desk_Reject_Pre_Flight_Checklist.md) ? A comprehensive, interactive Markdown audit covering:
  * *Scope alignment & structured abstract checks*
  * *Literature review recency (minimum 40% citations from 2023?2026)*
  * *Math formatting & grammatical equation punctuation*
  * *Figure resolution (300+ DPI) & three-line table rules*
  * *Active Crossref DOI verification and ethical compliance statements*

> ?? **Automate your pre-submission audit:**  
> Run your draft through **[ManuscriptOS Reviewer 2 Pre-Flight](https://manuscriptos.io/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=lead_magnet&utm_campaign=academic_publishing_toolkit)** to automatically scan 40+ editorial compliance metrics, verify all DOIs in real-time, and catch desk-reject triggers in under 60 seconds.

## ? 4. LaTeX to Word OMML Math Cheat Sheet
Retyping complex mathematical equations into Microsoft Word using the mouse-driven Equation Builder is excruciatingly slow.

* [**LaTeX to Word OMML Math Cheat Sheet**](./cheat_sheets/LaTeX_to_Word_OMML_CheatSheet.md) ? Complete guide to typing lightning-fast formulas using Word's built-in `Alt + =` linear math engine:
  * Fractions, roots, powers, and indices
  * Complete Greek alphabet shortcuts (`\alpha`, `\beta`, `\Gamma`, `\Omega`)
  * Calculus operators, contour integrals, and partial derivatives
  * Summations, products, limits, and piecewise functions
  * Native $N 	imes M$ matrix syntax (`[\matrix(a&b@c&d)]`)
  * The hidden right-aligned equation numbering trick (`#(1)`)

## ?? 5. Literature Gap & Baseline Synthesis Matrix
Reviewers universally reject literature reviews that merely list previous works sequentially (*"Author A proposed X. Author B introduced Y"*). Top Q1 journals require **thematic synthesis** with explicit comparative tables.

* [**Literature Gap Matrix Template (.md)**](./matrices/Literature_Gap_Matrix_Template.md) ? Standardized academic framework for recording problem objectives, methodology, evaluation benchmarks, and unresolved failure modes.
* [**Literature Gap Matrix Template (.csv)**](./matrices/Literature_Gap_Matrix_Template.csv) ? Spreadsheet template for Excel, Google Sheets, or Notion tracking.
