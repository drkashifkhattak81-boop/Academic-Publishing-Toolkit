## 🛠️ 3. How to Choose the Right Target Journal

1. **Avoid Desk-Rejections by Scrutinizing "Out of Scope" Precedents:**  
   Before submitting, search the journal's recent issues for papers that use your same methodology (e.g., search *"Transformers"* in *IEEE Access*). If zero similar papers appear in the last 12 months, the journal may not have active editorial reviewers in your specific subfield.
2. **Review Speed vs. Journal Prestige:**  
   * If you are a PhD student facing a graduation deadline, prioritized venues with rapid turnarounds (e.g., *IEEE Access*, *Sensors*, *Heliyon*) offer 4–6 week first-decision speeds.
   * If your manuscript introduces a landmark theoretical proof, top-tier transactions (e.g., *IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI)*) carry higher academic prestige but can take 4–9 months per review cycle.
3. **APC Waivers & Institutional Agreements:**  
   Many universities maintain transformational read-and-publish agreements with Springer Nature, Elsevier, and Wiley that waive 100% of Open Access Article Processing Charges (APCs). Always check your university library portal before paying APCs out of pocket.
